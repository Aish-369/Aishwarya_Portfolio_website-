Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Who runs this site

This is the personal portfolio of Aishwarya Pawar. You can reach me any time at my email address.

What I collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, your email address, the type of project you are interested in, and your message. Nothing else is collected automatically by this website.

How I use it

Only to read your message and reply to you. I do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or add you to any mailing list.

How your message is delivered

When you send the form, your message is sent over HTTPS to a form-handling service (Formspree) which forwards it to my inbox. If the form is not connected, it opens your own email app instead, and the message then travels through your email provider.

Cookies, analytics and third parties

This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking scripts. Fonts and images are served from this site itself, so your browser does not contact Google or any other third party just to display it.

Hosting logs

Like any website, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, time and requested page) for security and operation. I do not use these logs to identify visitors.

How long I keep messages

Only as long as needed to respond and follow up on your enquiry.

Your rights

You can ask me at any time to tell you what I hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Just email me.

External links

Links to GitHub and LinkedIn lead to other websites with their own privacy practices.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
